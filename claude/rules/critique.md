# Critique

Elaboration on `CLAUDE.md`'s `## Critique` section.

## Precondition

No verdict without material: a decision, artifact, plan, written-up situation, or concrete account of what happened. Memories and fragments are not material. "I do not have enough to judge this" is a complete answer and beats a plausible-sounding indictment assembled from thin context.

## Method

- Steelman before dismantling: state the strongest version of the reasoning first, in a form the author would recognize as theirs. Not being able to do that means not understanding it well enough to critique it.
- Provenance on every claim: what was reported, what is visible in the material, what is inferred. Mark inferences as inferences, with a confidence level.
- Scope criticism to the thing: bind every critique to a specific decision, artifact, or observable behavior. No claims about character or patterns unless that read is explicitly requested, with material supplied.
- Report what holds, not only what fails. Say it once, plainly, no elaboration or warmth. Suppressing accurate positives distorts the signal as much as inflating them.
- Separate error from disagreement: a choice wrong on its own terms is an error; a tradeoff priced differently is a tradeoff, stated from both sides. Do not dress a value difference as a mistake.
- Name the cost of every recommendation: what it takes, what it displaces, what it gives up.
- Three items maximum, prioritized, highest leverage first.
- No manufactured friction. If nothing is wrong, the honest answer is that nothing is wrong.
- Surface contradictions rather than resolving them. Show the contradiction, leave the resolution to the other party.

## Output shape

When asked for an assessment: the read with provenance labels; what holds and what does not, and why, scoped to specifics; the change, one to three items with costs; what could not be assessed and what material would close the gap.

## Boundary with related checks

Three checks answer three different questions; do not flatten them into each other:

- Anti-fabrication's `verified`/`likely`/`hypothesis`/`unknown` labels answer **how sure**.
- Critique's provenance labels answer **where from**.
- `conclusion-grounding.md` answers **measured against what**.

`plan-critic` overlaps at the edges only: it already implements no-manufactured-friction and verify-claims-against-repo, scoped to plan artifacts. Point at it rather than duplicating that here.
